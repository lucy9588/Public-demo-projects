Environment & Growth

Family Harmony

Growing up in a household that valued creativity and storytelling. Every dinner was a session of "what if?" and "how does it work?".

Academic Spark

From the first hand-drawn school project to advanced design studies, the environment was always supportive of experimental thinking.

Childhood Memories

A Curious Mind

I was the child who collected interesting pebbles and pressed flowers into journals. This innate desire to "archive" beauty led directly to my passion for scrapbooking and knowledge management today.

Scrapbook Essentials

  • Washi Tape: The colorful borders that frame my thoughts and keep ideas together.
  • Fountain Pens: For the tactile joy of seeing ink meet paper in a fluid dance.
  • Instax Mini: Capturing raw, unedited moments of pure joy and discovery.
  • Nature Finds: Pressed leaves and flowers that bring the outside world into my journal.
